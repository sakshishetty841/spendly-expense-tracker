Student Budgeting Guidelines

Rule of thumb for sustainable student finances

  • The 50/30/20 Rule: 50% for Needs (mess fees, books, transport), 30% for Wants (outings, shopping), and 20% for Savings or emergency cushion.
  • Weekly Sub-budgets: Divide your monthly limit by 4 to keep track of weekly spending velocity.
  • Group Expenses: For shared flat or hostel snacks, log your exact share to avoid hidden drain.
